Marketing in EB Master

The AI writes posts about your in-stock pieces, you approve them, and EB Master posts them for you. Keep this tab open and switch back to EB Master whenever you need it.

What Marketing does for you

Nothing ever goes live without your OK.

  • Three channels per piece: a Pinterest pin, an Instagram post and a Facebook post.
  • Your listing video is used when there is one. It becomes an Instagram Reel and a Facebook video.
  • It follows the season. It starts on Christmas in mid-September, Valentine's Day right after Christmas, and so on, the way stores do.
  • Each channel's voice: Instagram describes the piece with a light "handmade by Patti" touch. Facebook sounds like you talking and is signed "— Patti".
  • New drafts arrive every week on their own. The Marketing card on the Home screen shows how many are waiting, e.g. "5 to review".

Right now Facebook and Instagram post automatically. Pinterest is waiting for Pinterest to approve our app; until then, pins are posted by hand (see If something looks wrong).

Your weekly routine (about 5 minutes)

Once a week, open Marketing and approve or skip each waiting piece. That's all the system needs from you.

  1. Open EB Master and tap the Marketing card on the Home screen. The badge shows how many pieces are waiting.
  2. Stay on the To review tab. Each card is one piece, with its Pinterest, Instagram and Facebook posts stacked together.
  3. For each piece, read the text and look at the photo or video.
  4. Tap ✓ Approve & post all. Or tap Skip piece if you don't want to post it right now.
  5. That's it. The posts go out over the next few days by themselves.

If you'd like more to review, tap ✨ Draft 5 new pieces at any time. It takes a minute or two.

Reviewing a piece card

You can change any word before approving. Your edits are what gets posted.

What's on a card

  • Photo or video of the piece on the left. A video shows a small VIDEO tag; tap it to play.
  • Badges next to the EB number: New (first time posted) or Repost, plus the season it was written for, e.g. Christmas.
  • "Posted before" line: where and when this piece went out earlier, e.g. "📷 Sep 20 · 📌 Sep 12 (Fall)".
  • One block per channel:
    • 📌 Pinterest: a title, a description and a board.
    • 📷 Instagram: a caption.
    • 👍 Facebook: a post, with the shop link at the end.

A quick check before approving: does the text describe the piece correctly (colors, flowers, container)? The AI looks at the photo, but you know the piece best.

Planning posts yourself (optional)

The weekly batch handles things on its own, but the Plan posts box on the Marketing screen lets you choose what goes out. Pieces can be posted again: each repost is rewritten for the season, never a copy of the old post.

Coming up (seasonal suggestions)

This lists the seasons to promote right now, each with how many pieces fit, how many were never posted, and how many are ready to post again. Tap Draft 5 next to a season, e.g. Christmas, to get 5 pieces drafted with that angle.

☑ Pick pieces… (choose them yourself)

  1. Tap Pick pieces….
  2. Narrow the list with the filters: season or occasion, type (wreath, centerpiece…), Never posted or Ready to repost, or search by name or EB number.
  3. Tick up to 10 pieces. Each row shows when it was last posted on each channel.
  4. Under Post to, untick any channel you don't want.
  5. Under Angle, choose a season or occasion, or leave Whatever season fits now. You can also pick Everyday, Sympathy & memorial, or Weddings & showers.
  6. Tap ✨ Draft selected. The drafts appear in To review.

🎲 Surprise me

Tap it to get 5 random pieces that haven't been posted in a while.

How often a piece comes back

A piece can repost on the same channel 30 days after its last post there. If you pick a piece yourself, it doesn't have to wait.

After you approve

Approved posts are spread out to about one per channel per day, so your followers don't get five posts in a minute.

  • Approved tab: each channel shows 🗓 Scheduled for with a day and time. Usual times are about noon for Instagram, 2 pm for Facebook and 8 pm for Pinterest (Central).
  • Post now: tap it under a channel if you want that post out right away, e.g. for a craft show this weekend.
  • Back to drafts: changed your mind? Tap it before the scheduled time.
  • Posted tab: everything that went out, with View on Instagram / Facebook / Pinterest links.
  • Videos take a minute or two to process. You'll see "⏳ Posting…" meanwhile.

When a piece sells

You don't have to do anything:

  • Drafts and scheduled posts for that piece are skipped automatically.
  • Live Pinterest pins and Facebook posts are updated to point to similar pieces in the shop.
  • Instagram doesn't allow editing captions, so those posts stay as they are.

If something looks wrong

What you seeWhat to do
A red Couldn't post message on a channelTap ↻ Retry. If it fails again, take a screenshot and send it to Bob.
"Posted as a photo — the video failed" noteNothing. The post went out with the photo instead; the video was the problem, not you.
Pinterest says not connectedExpected for now. Tap 📌 Post on Pinterest, paste the title (it's copied for you), pick the board shown, publish, then tap Mark as posted.
Facebook & Instagram say not connectedSend a screenshot to Bob. It may need to be reconnected once.
Nothing to draft messageEvery matching piece is already waiting or was posted in the last 30 days. Use Pick pieces… to choose some anyway.
The text gets a fact wrongFix it in the box before approving, or tap Skip for that channel.

When in doubt, don't approve. A skipped piece can always come back later.
